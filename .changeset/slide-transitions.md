Play slide transitions in presentation and viewer mode. The `transition` directive works as in Marp CLI: the 33 built-in transitions (e.g. `transition: fade 1s`), custom transitions from project themes via `marp-transition-<name>` keyframes, backward-specific keyframes, `--marp-transition-duration` and `--marp-transition-direction`. Viewers who prefer reduced motion get a plain crossfade.
